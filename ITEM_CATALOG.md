- **Supernova:** 100 Star Gems initially, price and restock wait double after every purchase, starting with a one-hour wait. Permanently out of stock from 1 December 2026 at midnight AEDT. Owned ships stay playable. Nova Aegis starts with a 24-second cooldown and absorbs 1.5× max hull. Recharges 24 seconds after breaking.
- **Singularity:** Obtained from the Spaceship Crate. Barrage uses 115% energy if at least two-thirds of the wave remains. Fires 960 shots over 8 seconds in six passes over an 80° forward arc. Shots deal 1.25× base damage, benefit from boosts, and can hit three enemies or reflect off side walls. Rapid Fire at activation can raise the shot count up to 4×. A fitted Ultimate disables Barrage. The main gun pauses during Barrage.
- **Alien variants:** Any obtainable regular ship can be alienated once. Its rarity and energy cap carry over. Hull, movement speed, base damage, and firing rate are 1.1× the donor’s. Alien ships use tendrils and can gain Mind Control by crafting. They lose the donor’s native ultimate.

## December snowfall

- **Natural snowfall:** 10% chance at each tenth wave in December 2026, Melbourne time. Each snowfall lasts 18 seconds and releases 15–30 Common Snowflakes. The Mythical Snowman has a 1% chance per snowfall. It shines white as a pickup. Uncaught pickups fall offscreen.
- **Snow Crystal:** Rare, crafted with five Snowflakes in a plus pattern. Triggers snowfall from a boost slot, with a 90-second cooldown. Crafted crystals can summon snowfall only during December 2026.
- **Snowman:** Snowman and Alien Snowman fire homing snowballs, green for the Alien variant, that prioritize the lowest hostile enemies and reserve enough shots for their remaining health before assigning extra shots elsewhere. They retarget when their target disappears. Every 25th projectile is a crystal that targets the highest-HP hostile enemy and freezes it for eight seconds on impact, including bosses. Frozen enemies cannot fire, damage the ship or die from ship contact. Each projectile in a multi-shot volley counts. Snowfall boosts base hull, movement speed, damage and fire rate by 1.5×. Its energy cap stays at 105%.
- **Wave and run payouts:** Credits, Star Gems, and crates from finish rewards and milestones pay 25% of their previous quantities, rounded down. Wave-clear Credits follow the same reduction.


## Daily quests

- Unlocks at 3:00 p.m. AEDT on 28 November 2026. Quests reset at midnight Melbourne time. There are 30 daily objectives across enemies, loot, waves cleared, deepest wave cleared and bosses. Fresh pilots have 10 unlocked, with all 30 open at level 20. Complete objectives sequentially, then claim their rewards manually. Quests 11–29 pay 1 Star Gem each, with the milestone totals below replacing that amount for quests 10, 20 and 30.
- **Golden Trident:** Legendary single-use item-slot weapon from quest 20 with a 100-second cooldown between uses. Works on any wave. Golden arcs halve the health of spawned enemies and briefly display damage and health bars. Wave 50 spawns its bosses and regular enemies together. On that wave it can fire once, destroying every other hostile and halving the lead Nexus Overlord’s current health to expose the core. It deals 1.5× damage, moves 1.5× faster and fires at 1.5× cadence.
- **Energy Core:** Mythical material with the red Power Cell 1 icon. Drops from the lead Nexus Overlord after Golden Trident exposure. Catch it before it falls offscreen. Wave 50 waits for the core to be caught or leave the playfield.
- **Quest Supernova:** Once Premium Shop stock ends on 1 December, claim a Supernova for 1 Energy Core after completing all 30 quests on the same Melbourne day. 24-hour cooldown between claims.
- **Snow Crystal recipe:** Appears in Event Recipes from 1 December 2026 onward.

### Jolly Gift Bag

The Legendary Jolly Gift Bag uses the Holiday Gift Bag icon. Each natural December snowfall has a 10% chance to drop a bag. Open collected bags year-round from Inventory → Crates.

Every bag gives 50–250 Credits, 3–10 Star Gems and 5–15 Snowflakes, plus three independent random items from the game catalogue, including currencies, ships, all Mythical and Godly ships and their Alien variants, existing lower-rarity Alien variants, the bag itself and retired event rewards. Per-item weights are Common 1, Rare 0.6, Epic 0.3, Legendary 0.12, Mythical 0.05 and Godly 0.015. Items of the same rarity have equal odds.
